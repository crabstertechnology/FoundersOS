# FounderOS: The Ultimate Startup Operating System
### "Scale Your Startup with Analytical Rigor."

## 🚀 The Elevator Pitch
**FounderOS** is the virtual legal and financial co-founder for early-stage Indian startups. It empowers founders to instantly calculate their company's valuation, protect their equity mathematically, decode predatory VC term sheets using AI, and manage unit economics. We ensure founders never lose control of their companies to bad deals or mismanaged cap tables.

---

## ⚠️ The Problem
The Indian startup ecosystem is the 3rd largest globally, adding thousands of companies annually. However, most technical and product-focused founders lack deep financial and legal expertise. 
*   **Information Asymmetry:** VCs and institutional investors have armies of lawyers. Founders often sign terms they don't understand (e.g., *2x Participating Liquidation Preferences* or *Full-Ratchet Anti-Dilution*).
*   **Cap Table Chaos:** Many early-stage founders surrender too much equity in Seed/Series A rounds because they cannot properly model "What-If" dilution scenarios.
*   **Poor Unit Economics:** Startups burn cash without tracking exact Break-Even points, LTV over CAC ratios, and exact Runway limits.

## 💡 The Solution (Our Core Products)
1. **Valuation Engine:** Automated Pre & Post-Money modeling using standard VC methods and revenue multiples. Features a "Survival Dashboard" that grades unit economics (LTV:CAC ratio) to prove business models work.
2. **Equity Tracker (Cap Table):** A proprietor-centric dilution model. Founders can track every share explicitly, view changes round-by-round, and simulate ESOP pool creations before agreeing to new investments.
3. **Term Sheet AI:** A virtual legal co-founder. Founders paste standard clauses, and the AI highlights predatory liquidation preferences, red-flags aggressive anti-dilution, and generates founder-friendly counter-arguments instantly.
4. **Founder's Glossary:** Translates complex VC jargon (e.g., "Pro-Rata Rights") into plain English with practical Indian market (INR) examples.
