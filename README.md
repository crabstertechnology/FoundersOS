# FounderOS — Startup Toolkit for Indian Founders

FounderOS is a professional-grade strategic platform designed specifically for the Indian startup ecosystem. It empowers founders to transition from being "Product-focused" to "CFO-ready" by automating complex financial modeling, equity tracking, and unit economics analysis.

## Key Features

### 1. Startup Valuation Calculator
*   **VC-Method Modeling**: Calculate implied pre-money and post-money valuations based on investment amount and equity offered.
*   **Revenue Multiples**: Instantly compare your valuation against industry-standard multiples (SaaS, AI, Fintech, EdTech, etc.).
*   **Deal Term Analysis**: Evaluate the impact of Liquidation Preference Multiples (1x, 2x) and Types (Participating vs. Non-Participating) on your final exit proceeds.

### 2. Survival Dashboard: Unit Economics
*   **LTV (Lifetime Value)**: Tracks profit-based LTV (Profit per Order × Orders per Customer) to show true customer value.
*   **CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost)**: Monitors the efficiency of your sales and marketing spend.
*   **LTV:CAC Ratio**: Real-time health checks (Target >3x) to ensure your business model is sustainable.
*   **Break-even CAC**: Identifies the exact maximum spend allowed per customer acquisition before the business becomes unprofitable.

### 3. Cap Table Tracker (Proprietorship Edition)
*   **Founder-Centric Dilution**: Logic optimized for proprietorships where the founder starts at 100%. Every partner or investor added automatically deducts from the primary stake.
*   **Partner Registry**: Manage ownership percentages for co-founders, investors, advisors, and ESOP pools.
*   **Funding History**: Record historical investment milestones (Seed, Series A, etc.) including capital raised and equity granted.
*   **Dilution Engine**: A "What-If" simulator to project the impact of new grants on the founder's remaining stake.

### 4. Founder's Glossary
*   **Plain-English Definitions**: Master complex VC jargon like "Full Ratchet Anti-Dilution," "ROFR," and "Tag-Along Rights."
*   **Indian Context**: Real-world examples using INR (₹) and figures relevant to the Tamil Nadu and broader Indian startup landscape.
*   **Strategic Warnings**: Highlights "Dark Patterns" and high-risk clauses in term sheets that founders should avoid.

### 5. AI Strategic Advisor
*   **Contextual Analysis**: Generates personalized reports by analyzing your unique cap table health and unit economics.
*   **Strengths & Warnings**: Identifies critical risks (e.g., low runway, high CAC, or excessive dilution) and highlights operational strengths.
*   **Actionable Recommendations**: Provides a prioritized list of next steps for growth and founder protection.

### 6. Quick Utility Calculator
*   **Built-in Access**: A floating arithmetic tool available in the header for fast calculations without switching tabs.
*   **Operation History**: Displays the previous value and current operator for error-free math.
*   **Keyboard Support**: Optimized for speed with full numpad and keyboard interaction support.

## Tech Stack
*   **Frontend**: Next.js 15 (App Router), React 19, Tailwind CSS, ShadCN UI.
*   **Backend/Database**: Firebase Firestore (Real-time data synchronization).
*   **Authentication**: Firebase Auth (Secured founder access).
*   **AI Engine**: Genkit with Google Gemini 2.5 Flash for strategic modeling.

## Getting Started
1.  **Founder Access**: Sign in with authorized credentials to unlock your dashboard.
2.  **Model Your Unit Economics**: Enter your revenue, burn, and purchase metrics in the Calculator to see your LTV/CAC health.
3.  **Audit Your Cap Table**: Registry your partners and simulate dilution to ensure you maintain control.
4.  **Master the Jargon**: Use the Glossary before any investor meeting to understand exactly what you are signing.
5.  **Consult the AI**: Generate a Strategic Report to get an outside-in perspective on your startup's viability.
