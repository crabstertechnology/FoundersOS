# **App Name**: FounderOS

## Core Features:

- Startup Valuation Calculator: Calculate company valuation using VC method, Revenue Multiples, and Berkus Score based on key financial inputs and market data. Includes alerts for high investment dilution or unrealistic valuations.
- Equity Management & Dilution Visualizer: Visually represent current equity distribution among founders, investors, ESOP, and advisors. Simulate dilution across multiple funding rounds to understand future ownership scenarios and highlight potential control issues.
- Financial Health Dashboard: Monitor critical financial metrics including runway, LTV:CAC ratio, and CAC payback period. Provides immediate feedback and alerts on key health indicators like burn rate vs. revenue.
- Cap Table Management: Manage shareholders, track share types (common, preference, ESOP), and visualize ownership percentages dynamically. Automatically highlights cap table errors and sums to ensure 100% allocation.
- Founder Authority Health Checks: Analyzes the cap table to identify potential risks to founder voting majority, investor preference share dominance, or ESOP pool balance, providing actionable tips for protection.
- Interactive Startup Glossary: A comprehensive and searchable glossary of critical startup terms, providing plain English descriptions, real-world examples with financial figures, and India-specific insights, categorized for easy navigation.
- Contextual Startup Advice Generator: Leverages an AI tool to generate personalized strategic advice and early warnings based on the aggregated financial calculations, equity structures, and health checks, helping founders make informed decisions.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary brand color: A deep, professional blue (#1f4fad) for trust and analytical rigor, contrasting well with light backgrounds.
- Background color: A very subtle, cool-toned off-white (#f2f6fa) to maintain a clean, expansive, and focused interface.
- Accent color: A vibrant cyan-blue (#0fe4e8) for highlighting interactive elements, calls to action, and drawing attention to critical data points, providing a fresh contrast to the primary blue.
- Headline font: 'Syne' (sans-serif) for its strong, modern aesthetic. Body font: 'DM Sans' (sans-serif) for legibility and a contemporary feel. Monospace font: 'DM Mono' (monospace) for displaying data and code-like numerical values. Note: currently only Google Fonts are supported.
- Utilize minimalist, line-art style icons that complement the clean interface, employing brand colors for key actions and status indicators (e.g., green for positive, red for alerts).
- Emphasize a card-based layout with clear spatial separation for different sections and data visualizations, promoting scannability and modularity. Employ grid structures for responsive arrangement of inputs and results.
- Subtle and functional animations for state changes (e.g., button hovers, chart updates, accordion expands) to provide intuitive feedback without distracting from the data.
